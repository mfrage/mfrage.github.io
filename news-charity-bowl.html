AHERO Skeet Shooting Event Raises Funds for Veterans

October 15, 2025 Delta Alpha Chapter

The Delta Alpha Chapter proudly partnered with AHERO (Afghanistan's Health, Education and Relief Organization) for an exceptional skeet shooting fundraiser that raised significant funds to support veterans and medical relief efforts. This meaningful event demonstrates our chapter's unwavering commitment to service and honoring those who have served our nation.

Held at a premier shooting range near Auburn, the event brought together brothers, alumni, students, and community members for a day of competitive clay shooting, camaraderie, and giving back to a worthy cause. This partnership with AHERO has become a treasured tradition that showcases DKE's dedication to supporting veterans and humanitarian relief efforts.

About AHERO

AHERO is a remarkable organization dedicated to providing medical services, supplies, and relief to Afghanistan while also supporting veterans and medical initiatives. Founded by Dr. Aziz Kamali, AHERO has spent over 28 years collecting donated medical supplies from hospitals and clinics to ship to Afghanistan, working to establish the Omar Kamali Medical Center in Jalalabad.

The organization's mission includes providing medical services, artificial limbs to those in need, establishing an orphanage, and creating facilities that operate as combat-free zones. 100% of donated finances and materials directly benefit those in need.

Event Highlights

This year's AHERO Skeet Shooting Event featured:

  • Competitive Skeet Shooting Tournament with multiple skill levels
  • Clay Shooting Challenges and Target Competitions
  • Safety Briefings and Professional Range Officers
  • Alumni Participation and Mentorship
  • Raffle Prizes including shooting equipment and Auburn memorabilia
  • Barbecue Lunch and Brotherhood Fellowship
  • Silent Auction featuring outdoor and sporting goods

Supporting Veterans and Medical Relief

The funds raised through this event support AHERO's critical mission to:

  • Provide Medical Services - Supporting healthcare delivery in underserved regions
  • Support Veterans - Assisting those who have served with medical and humanitarian aid
  • Medical Equipment and Supplies - Collecting and shipping donated medical materials
  • Emergency Medical Services - Establishing facilities to provide urgent care
  • Artificial Limbs Program - Providing prosthetics to those in need

Brotherhood and Service

The success of this year's event reflects the dedication of our entire chapter and the strong support from our alumni network. Many of our alumni, including veterans themselves, participated in the event, sharing their expertise in shooting sports while connecting with current members and supporting a cause close to their hearts.

Special recognition goes to our Philanthropy Committee for their meticulous planning and coordination. Their commitment to organizing a safe, professional, and impactful event exemplifies the values of Delta Kappa Epsilon and our dedication to serving those who have served.

A Tradition of Service

The AHERO Skeet Shooting Fundraiser is a cornerstone of the Delta Alpha Chapter's philanthropic efforts. Our members take pride in supporting veterans and humanitarian causes, recognizing that true brotherhood means standing with those who have sacrificed for others.

As we celebrate another successful event, we are already planning for next year and exploring additional ways to support AHERO's mission. The Delta Alpha Chapter remains committed to making a meaningful difference in the lives of veterans and those in need of medical assistance.

We invite community members, veterans, and shooting enthusiasts to join us for next year's event. Together, we can continue honoring those who serve and supporting AHERO's vital humanitarian work.

About the Delta Alpha Chapter

Delta Kappa Epsilon was founded at Yale University in 1844 and is one of the oldest and most prestigious fraternities in North America. DKE is dedicated to the cultivation of a unique environment which instills a lifelong commitment to brotherhood based on the values of Friendship, Justice, and Learning.

The Delta Alpha Chapter of Delta Kappa Epsilon was established at Auburn University in 2019, making us one of the oldest and most prestigious fraternities on campus. Our chapter embodies the timeless values of our founders.

We are committed to developing men of character who contribute positively to Auburn's community and beyond through excellence in academics, leadership, service, and brotherhood.

Message from Our President

Jack Hoblock

Albany, NY

Brothers and prospective members,

It is my honor to serve as President of the Delta Alpha Chapter of Delta Kappa Epsilon. Our chapter has a rich history at Auburn University, spanning over 175 years of excellence in brotherhood, scholarship, and service.

We are committed to upholding the values of Friendship, Justice, and Learning that have guided our fraternity since its founding. Our brothers come from diverse backgrounds but are united in their dedication to personal growth, academic excellence, and making a positive impact on the Auburn community.

Whether you're a prospective member, alumnus, or friend of DKE, I invite you to learn more about our chapter and the opportunities we offer for leadership development, lifelong friendships, and meaningful service.

War Eagle and Friends from the Heart,
Jack Hoblock
President, Delta Alpha Chapter

Community Partners

The Delta Alpha Chapter is dedicated to making a lasting impact on the Auburn community and beyond. Through our annual AHERO skeet shooting fundraiser, local volunteer initiatives, and partnerships with charitable organizations, we embody the value of Justice by serving those in need.

Dogs at DKE

Our chapter partners with local animal shelters to host "Dogs at DKE" events where students can de-stress with therapy dogs while raising awareness and funds for animal rescue organizations in the Auburn area.

Read More

AHERO Skeet Shooting

Our annual skeet shooting event raises funds for AHERO.org, supporting veterans and medical relief efforts. This signature event brings together brothers, alumni, and the community for a day of shooting sports and service.

Read More

Movember

Each November, our brothers grow mustaches to raise awareness and funds for men's health issues including prostate cancer, testicular cancer, and mental health. We're proud to support the Movember Foundation's mission.

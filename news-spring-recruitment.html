DKE Chapter Excels in Spring Recruitment

January 15, 2026 Delta Alpha Chapter

The Delta Alpha Chapter of Delta Kappa Epsilon had an outstanding spring recruitment period, welcoming a diverse and exceptional class of new members who embody our fraternity's core values of Friendship, Justice, and Learning.

This semester's recruitment efforts focused on identifying young men who demonstrate academic excellence, leadership potential, and a commitment to service. Our chapter hosted a series of events designed to showcase the unique brotherhood and opportunities that DKE offers at Auburn University.

Recruitment Events

Throughout the recruitment period, we organized various activities including:

  • Meet and Greet Sessions with current members and alumni
  • Campus Tours and Academic Success Workshops
  • Community Service Projects
  • Social Events and Brotherhood Activities
  • Information Sessions about DKE's History and Values

New Member Class

We are proud to welcome our newest class of brothers who represent diverse backgrounds, academic disciplines, and interests. Each new member brings unique talents and perspectives that will strengthen our chapter and contribute to Auburn's campus community.

Our new members have already begun participating in chapter activities, including philanthropy events, study sessions, and leadership development programs. They are enthusiastic about upholding DKE's legacy of excellence and making positive contributions to both our fraternity and the Auburn community.

Looking Forward

The successful recruitment season reflects the dedication of our recruitment chairs, Ford Pridgen and George Simmons, as well as the entire chapter's commitment to finding the best candidates to join our brotherhood. We are confident that this new class will uphold our values and continue the tradition of excellence that defines the Delta Alpha Chapter.

As we move forward into the semester, we look forward to integrating our new members into all aspects of chapter life and watching them grow as leaders, scholars, and brothers.

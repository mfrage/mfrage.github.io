Dogs at DKE: De-Stress with Therapy Dogs

Ongoing Event Delta Alpha Chapter

Dogs at DKE is one of our chapter's most beloved philanthropy initiatives, bringing together students, therapy dogs, and community support for local animal rescue organizations. This event has become a staple during high-stress periods of the semester, providing Auburn students with a much-needed break while raising awareness and funds for animals in need.

The Mission

Our partnership with local animal shelters and rescue organizations serves a dual purpose: providing stress relief for Auburn students while supporting the critical work of animal welfare organizations in the Lee County area. We believe that combining mental wellness support with charitable giving creates a powerful impact on our community.

Event Highlights

Dogs at DKE events feature:

  • Certified therapy dogs from local organizations and trained volunteers
  • Interactive play sessions and photo opportunities with the dogs
  • Information booths about animal adoption and volunteer opportunities
  • Fundraising activities including donation drives and bake sales
  • Meet-and-greets with shelter representatives and adoptable pets
  • Educational materials about responsible pet ownership

Community Impact

Since launching Dogs at DKE, our chapter has:

  • Raised over $5,000 for local animal shelters and rescue organizations
  • Provided stress relief to hundreds of Auburn students during finals and midterms
  • Helped facilitate multiple pet adoptions through our events
  • Donated supplies including food, toys, and cleaning materials to shelters
  • Recruited dozens of new volunteers for local animal welfare organizations

Partner Organizations

We are proud to partner with several outstanding animal welfare organizations including:

  • Lee County Humane Society - Providing shelter and adoption services
  • East Alabama Animal Rescue - Rescuing and rehoming animals in need
  • Auburn Therapy Dogs - Offering certified therapy dog services
  • AU Vet School Volunteers - Supporting animal health education

Get Involved

Dogs at DKE events are held multiple times each semester, typically during midterm and final exam periods. All members of the Auburn community are welcome to attend, and we encourage students to bring donations for our partner shelters. Whether you're looking to de-stress, support a good cause, or potentially meet your new best friend, Dogs at DKE offers something for everyone.

For information about upcoming Dogs at DKE events or to learn how you can support our partnership with local animal welfare organizations, please contact our Philanthropy Committee or follow us on social media for event announcements.

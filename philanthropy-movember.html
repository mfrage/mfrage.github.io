Movember: Growing Awareness for Men's Health

Every November Delta Alpha Chapter

Every November, the Delta Alpha Chapter joins millions of people around the world in supporting Movember - a global movement raising awareness and funds for men's health issues. Through growing mustaches, hosting awareness events, and fundraising campaigns, our brothers work to address critical health challenges facing men today: prostate cancer, testicular cancer, mental health, and suicide prevention.

The Movember Mission

Movember is the leading charity changing the face of men's health on a global scale. The foundation addresses some of the biggest health issues facing men: prostate cancer, testicular cancer, mental health and suicide prevention. Since 2003, Movember has funded more than 1,250 men's health projects around the world, challenging the status quo, shaking up research, and transforming the way health services reach and support men.

Why Men's Health Matters

The statistics around men's health are sobering:

  • Men die on average 6 years earlier than women
  • 1 in 8 men will be diagnosed with prostate cancer in their lifetime
  • Testicular cancer is the most common cancer in young men
  • Three quarters of suicides are men
  • Men are far less likely to seek help for mental health issues
  • Young men aged 18-24 are at highest risk for mental health challenges

As a fraternity, we recognize the importance of addressing these issues within our own community and beyond. The culture of brotherhood provides a unique opportunity to break down barriers around men's mental health and encourage open conversations about health challenges.

Our Movember Campaign

Throughout November, the Delta Alpha Chapter runs a comprehensive Movember campaign that includes:

Growing Mustaches

Our brothers start the month clean-shaven and spend November growing mustaches to spark conversations about men's health. These "Mo's" become conversation starters that help break down stigma and raise awareness on campus and in the community.

Educational Events

  • Health Awareness Workshops: Partnering with Auburn Student Health to provide education on men's health screenings and prevention
  • Mental Health Panels: Featuring counselors, alumni, and student speakers sharing experiences and resources
  • Fitness Challenges: Encouraging physical activity through chapter-wide competitions and group workouts
  • Screening Information: Distributing materials on testicular self-exams and when to see a doctor

Fundraising Activities

  • Mo-ments Campaign: Online fundraising through our official Movember team page
  • Mustache Monday Socials: Weekly themed events with entry donations
  • Shave-Down Party: End-of-month celebration with shaving stations and donations
  • Campus Tabling: Information booths with donation collection at high-traffic areas
  • Alumni Challenge: Matching donation campaigns from DKE alumni
  • Movember Merch: T-shirts, hats, and accessories with proceeds going to the foundation

Physical Activity Challenge

In addition to growing mustaches, our chapter participates in Movember's MOVE challenge, committing to run or walk 60 miles over the month - representing the 60 men we lose to suicide every hour around the world. Brothers track their mileage through group runs, gym sessions, and individual activities, holding each other accountable while staying active.

Mental Health Focus

A key component of our Movember campaign is destigmatizing mental health conversations among college men. Through our partnership with Auburn's Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS), we:

  • Host "Real Talk" sessions where brothers share mental health experiences
  • Distribute mental health resources and crisis hotline information
  • Train chapter members in mental health first aid
  • Create peer support systems within the fraternity
  • Promote campus mental health services and reduce barriers to seeking help
  • Share personal stories that normalize therapy and counseling

Impact & Results

Since beginning our Movember participation, the Delta Alpha Chapter has:

  • Raised over $8,000 for the Movember Foundation
  • Engaged 100% of active members in awareness activities
  • Hosted mental health workshops attended by 200+ Auburn students
  • Distributed 1,000+ informational brochures on men's health
  • Completed over 2,000 cumulative miles in the MOVE challenge
  • Connected dozens of students with campus mental health resources
  • Built lasting partnerships with Auburn Student Health and CAPS

Breaking the Stigma

Movember provides an opportunity to challenge traditional masculine stereotypes that prevent men from seeking help. By openly discussing mental health, encouraging vulnerability, and normalizing healthcare check-ups, we work to create a culture where men feel comfortable prioritizing their health and wellbeing.

The fraternity environment - traditionally associated with masculinity - becomes a powerful platform for change when brothers lead by example. Seeing friends and respected leaders discuss their own health challenges creates permission for others to do the same.

Get Involved

There are many ways to support our Movember campaign:

  • Grow a Mustache: Join the movement and spark conversations
  • Donate: Support our team fundraising page online
  • Participate: Join our MOVE challenge and track your miles
  • Attend Events: Come to our awareness workshops and social events
  • Share: Spread the word on social media using #Movember and #DKEMovember
  • Start a Conversation: Talk openly about men's health with friends

Resources

If you or someone you know is struggling with mental health, these resources are available:

  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • Auburn CAPS: (334) 844-5123
  • Movember Resources: us.movember.com
  • Mental Health First Aid: Information available at our chapter house

November may be Movember, but our commitment to men's health extends year-round. Through continued awareness, open conversations, and supportive brotherhood, we're working to change the face of men's health one mustache - and one conversation - at a time.

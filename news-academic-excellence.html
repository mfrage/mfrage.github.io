Chapter Receives Academic Excellence Award

December 10, 2025 Delta Alpha Chapter

The Delta Alpha Chapter of Delta Kappa Epsilon has been recognized with the Academic Excellence Award for maintaining one of the highest GPAs among fraternities at Auburn University for the fall semester. This prestigious honor highlights our chapter's commitment to the value of Learning, one of the three pillars of our fraternity.

Our chapter achieved a cumulative GPA of 3.45 for the fall 2025 semester, placing us among the top-performing Greek organizations on campus. This achievement reflects the dedication of our members to academic success and our chapter's strong culture of scholarship.

Academic Support Programs

The Delta Alpha Chapter has implemented several programs to support our members' academic success:

  • Weekly Study Sessions in our chapter house
  • Peer Tutoring Program connecting members across disciplines
  • Academic Achievement Recognition at chapter meetings
  • Scholarship Chairman who coordinates study resources
  • Quiet Study Hours policy during exam periods
  • Faculty Mentorship Program

Individual Achievements

Several of our brothers earned individual academic honors this semester, including Dean's List recognition, departmental awards, and research grants. Our members are pursuing degrees across Auburn's colleges, including Engineering, Business, Liberal Arts, Sciences, and Agriculture.

We are particularly proud of the members who achieved perfect 4.0 GPAs this semester, demonstrating that fraternity membership and academic excellence are not only compatible but mutually reinforcing.

Commitment to Excellence

This award represents more than just high grades—it reflects our chapter's holistic approach to developing well-rounded individuals who excel in all aspects of college life. Our members understand that academic achievement is fundamental to personal growth and future success.

As we celebrate this recognition, we remain committed to maintaining high academic standards and supporting every member in reaching their full potential. The Delta Alpha Chapter will continue to prioritize scholarship alongside our other core values of Friendship and Justice.

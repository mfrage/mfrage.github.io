PHILANTHROPY

AHERO Skeet Shooting: Supporting Veterans & Medical Relief

October 15, 2025 Delta Alpha Chapter

The AHERO (Afghanistan's Health, Education and Relief Organization) Skeet Shooting event stands as the Delta Alpha Chapter's flagship philanthropy initiative. This annual fundraiser combines the excitement of competitive clay shooting with meaningful support for veterans and critical medical relief efforts in Afghanistan. Since partnering with AHERO, we've raised over $15,000 while bringing the Auburn community together for an unforgettable day of sport and service.

About AHERO

AHERO is a remarkable 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that provides essential medical supplies, surgical equipment, and educational resources to underserved regions of Afghanistan. Founded by veterans who served in the region, AHERO works directly with local healthcare providers to deliver life-saving resources where they're needed most. Their mission focuses on sustainable healthcare development and supporting communities affected by decades of conflict.

Event Overview

Our annual AHERO Skeet Shooting event features:

  • Competitive Tournament: Multiple divisions for all skill levels from beginners to experienced shooters
  • Professional Instruction: Certified instructors provide safety training and shooting tips
  • Team Competition: Fraternity, sorority, and community teams compete for top honors
  • Prizes & Awards: Trophies and prizes for top shooters and teams
  • Vendor Village: Local businesses and sponsors showcase their products
  • BBQ & Social: Post-competition meal and fellowship
  • Silent Auction: Premium items donated by local businesses and alumni
  • Veteran Speakers: AHERO founders and veterans share their experiences

Impact & Achievements

Through our partnership with AHERO, the Delta Alpha Chapter has made a significant impact:

  • Raised over $15,000 in direct donations to AHERO since our first event
  • Attracted 100+ participants annually from Auburn University and the local community
  • Partnered with 15+ local businesses as sponsors and supporters
  • Provided medical equipment serving thousands of patients in rural Afghanistan
  • Supported surgical missions that have performed hundreds of life-saving procedures
  • Raised awareness about veteran-led humanitarian efforts
  • Created lasting connections between the Auburn community and AHERO's mission

Event Details

The 2025 AHERO Skeet Shooting event will take place on October 15, 2025 at the Auburn Sportsmen's Club. Registration is open to individuals and teams, with early bird pricing available through September 15th.

All skill levels are welcome - no prior shooting experience is required. Our professional instructors provide comprehensive safety training and guidance throughout the day. Equipment rentals are available on-site for those who don't have their own gear.

Why AHERO Matters

AHERO's work goes beyond immediate medical relief. By partnering with local healthcare providers and focusing on sustainable development, AHERO helps build long-term healthcare infrastructure in regions devastated by conflict. The organization's veteran-led approach ensures that aid reaches those who need it most, with deep cultural understanding and operational efficiency.

For many Afghan families, AHERO's support represents their only access to modern medical care. The supplies and equipment we help provide through this event literally save lives - treating war injuries, supporting maternal health, and addressing chronic medical conditions that would otherwise go untreated.

Get Involved

There are many ways to support this important cause:

  • Participate: Register as an individual or team for the tournament
  • Sponsor: Business sponsorship opportunities available at multiple levels
  • Volunteer: Help with event setup, registration, and operations
  • Donate: Direct donations to AHERO accepted year-round
  • Spread the Word: Share event information on social media

For registration information, sponsorship opportunities, or to learn more about supporting AHERO through the Delta Alpha Chapter, please contact our Philanthropy Committee. Together, we're making a real difference in the lives of veterans and the communities they continue to serve.

Learn more about AHERO's mission at www.ahero.org
